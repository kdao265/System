# Product vision

SYSTEM V1 brings personal commitments, growth, knowledge, finances, and daily life into a coherent Personal Life OS. It exists to help its owner turn goals into action, understand effort and progress, and retain evidence of what they have achieved.

Gamification makes progress legible through quests, EXP, levels, stats, and achievements. It should encourage purposeful action rather than reward endless task accumulation. EXP expresses game progression; it is not money or a financial balance.

Productivity must be balanced with recovery. Workload and energy estimates, daily check-ins, and recovery quests help the owner adapt plans. Failure is information and does not automatically require punishment. Justified overload can waive penalties, and penalties must never recursively escalate.

Health features estimate productivity and recovery needs; they do not diagnose medical conditions. Verified evidence of achievements remains distinguishable from estimated progress.

Game/anime System interfaces provide inspiration, while SYSTEM V1 develops its own visual identity. The Product Owner controls priorities; new ideas are captured without continually expanding the first release.
