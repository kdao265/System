## Concepts future flows must preserve

Quest completion is distinct from evidence verification. Activity participation may relate to multiple criteria. Failure may lead to a waiver rather than a penalty. Recovery estimates, EXP and financial balances must be clearly identified. Integration failures should be distinguishable from application data state.

These are constraints for future flow proposals, not a full Quest specification or an approved screen inventory.
